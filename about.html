About DoseMyTank

DoseMyTank is a free web app for planted aquarium keepers who mix their own fertilizers or want to know exactly what a commercial fertilizer adds to their tank. It started as a Polish-language tool for local planted tank groups. Since version 8 it's fully available in English, with Polish as a second language.

It's an independent, non-commercial project: no ads, no paid version, no account and no tracking. It isn't affiliated with any fertilizer manufacturer or shop.

How the calculations work

  • Molar masses are calculated from chemical formulas using IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights – never typed in by hand. Every hydrate is a separate form, so MgSO₄·7H₂O and anhydrous MgSO₄ give the same amount of magnesium from different grams.
  • Concentrations are in mg/L. In fresh water 1 mg/L = 1 ppm. Hardness uses the German degree: 1 °dH = 10 mg/L CaO (7.15 mg/L Ca or 4.34 mg/L Mg).
  • Solubility is checked against values for 20 °C, with a safety margin when several salts share an ion (the common-ion effect).
  • Incompatible salts are split into separate bottles: calcium with phosphate or sulfate, iron and other metals with phosphate, ammonium + magnesium + phosphate (struvite), metals with carbonates.
  • CO₂ is estimated from pH and KH (CO₂ = 3 · KH · 10(7 − pH)) and shown as a band that reflects the accuracy of drop tests. Free ammonia uses Emerson et al. (1975).
  • Commercial fertilizers use the composition published by the manufacturer or printed on the label, with the source noted. Products without a full published composition are marked as approximate.
  • The calculation engine is covered by automated tests, including checks against hand-calculated reference values and the published PPS-Pro and EI numbers.

What the app doesn't do: it doesn't predict how much your plants will take up, and it doesn't replace water tests. Treat recommended doses as a starting point and adjust them after testing.

Your data

  • Everything you enter – tanks, tests, recipes – is stored only in your browser on your device.
  • The app doesn't send your data anywhere and doesn't use cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • The information pages (not the app itself) use Cloudflare Web Analytics – an anonymous visit counter without cookies that doesn't track individual visitors. It only shows how many people visit and from where (e.g. country, referring site).
  • You can save a backup file and load it on another device in Settings. Clearing your browser data deletes what's stored in the app.

Languages and units

The app follows your browser language on first launch (English or Polish) and remembers your choice; you can switch at any time in Settings, even offline. Calculations use liters, milliliters and grams; when entering a tank volume you can convert from US gallons.
