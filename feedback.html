Feedback and bug reports

Feedback goes through GitHub Issues, where every report stays visible and you can follow what happens to it. A free GitHub account is needed to post.

Report a bug →Something doesn't work, a screen looks broken or a number seems wrong. Suggest a feature →A missing tool, a fertilizer to add to the library, a better explanation.

What makes a report useful

  • Device and browser, e.g. “Pixel 7, Chrome” or “iPhone 13, Safari, installed on the home screen”.
  • App version – shown at the bottom of Settings (gear icon).
  • What you did and what happened, step by step. A screenshot helps a lot.
  • For a calculation: tap “Copy result” or “Copy recipe” in the app and paste the text – it contains the tank volume, salts, bottle and dose. If you think a number is wrong, say what you expected and where it comes from (label, test, another calculator).
  • For a translation: the screen and the exact sentence, plus how you'd say it.

Please don't post personal data. Reports are public.

Before you report

  • Check the FAQ – e.g. hydrate forms (MgSO₄ vs MgSO₄·7H₂O) explain most “wrong gram” reports.
  • If the app behaves oddly after an update, close it completely and open it again – the new version loads on the next start.
